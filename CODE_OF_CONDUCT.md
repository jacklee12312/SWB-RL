# Code of conduct

Project discussions should be constructive, technically grounded, and
welcoming to contributors of different experience levels.

- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Provide reproducible evidence for rules and training claims.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information
  about another participant.
- Respect copyright, attribution, privacy, and community data sources.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when behavior repeatedly harms the project or its community.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
